HabitBoost is a simple web-based habit tracking application designed to help users build small, positive daily habits. 
Many people find it difficult to stay consistent with habits because goals can feel too big, or they lose motivation over time. 
A lot of existing apps also make things more complicated by adding too many features or competitive elements.
From my research, I found that habit formation depends more on repetition than motivation, and that small, manageable actions 
are more sustainable over time. Therefore, HabitBoost focuses on simplicity, reminders, and light motivational features such as 
streaks and badges to support long-term consistency rather than short-term pressure.
The main users of the system are individuals who want to improve their daily routines, such as students or working professionals. 
Users can create habits, mark them as completed, and track their progress over time. The goal of this project is to show how ideas 
from habit research, such as repetition and micro-habits, can be applied in a simple and practical system. The design also considers 
usability, accessibility, and user wellbeing.
